About

My purpose is simply to make cool things with amazing people.

I’m a design-led maker. I build brands, products, motion and websites, crafted by hand and at agentic scale.

As Founding Designer at Alex AI (YC W24), I’m the creative lead for brand, digital, GTM, and customer-facing product. My workflow runs across Figma, Adobe CC, Claude Code, and other agentic tools, producing at the cadence of a full design team.

More and more of what I make is built rather than mocked up: product animations that run as live code, systems that let anyone on the team generate polished, on-brand creative, and this website, which I designed in Figma and built with Claude.

Before Alex, I led brand and marketing design at Fandom, growing the team from 3 to 15 while supporting content, product, and GTM. We delivered global rebrands of Fandom and GameSpot, designed dozens of unique sales and content products, and drove $20M+ in attributed revenue. I introduced AI workflows early, enabling every designer to produce at speed and scale.

Prior to Fandom, I worked agency-side and taught design at the Academy of Art University. Clients included Apple, Adobe, Stanford, and The North Face. My work earned 50+ industry awards, including recognition from AIGA, Communication Arts, and the Type Directors Club.
