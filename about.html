About

My purpose is simply to make cool things with amazing people.

(Also welcome: amazing things with cool people)

I’m a design-led maker. I work at agentic scale for brands and products, business and culture.

As Founding Designer at Alex AI (YC W24), I’m the creative lead for brand, digital, GTM, and customer-facing product. My process is AI-native, with hands-on work for precision and polish. As the space between idea and execution approaches zero, I operate at the cadence of a full creative team. A design system is the way a team produces assets, and what used to be a prototype is now the deliverable.

Before Alex, I was head of brand and marketing design at Fandom, where I grew the team from 3 to 15, led global rebrands of Fandom and GameSpot, and drove $20M+ in attributed revenue. I introduced AI workflows early, enabling the team to move faster and operate at greater scale.

Earlier, I worked agency-side and taught design at the university level. Clients included Apple, Adobe, Stanford, and The North Face. My work earned 50+ industry awards, including recognition from AIGA, Communication Arts, and the Type Directors Club.
