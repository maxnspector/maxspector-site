Fandom

Produced end to end by my team at Fandom, the company’s brand identity targets the consumer audience with the fun, excitement, and boundless joy that come with a love of imagined worlds. To the business audience it tells the story of Fandom’s massive, global reach, its platform, and a diverse user base.
